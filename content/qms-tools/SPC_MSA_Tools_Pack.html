Western Electric / Nelson Control Rules
Reference guide for standard SPC out-of-control signals. Use these rules when interpreting your control charts.
RuleSignal DescriptionPatternAction
Rule 1One point beyond 3σ control limitsAny single point outside UCL or LCLInvestigate immediately — special cause likely
Rule 29 consecutive points on same side of centerline9 in a row above or below X̄Process shift — check for material, operator, or machine change
Rule 36 consecutive points steadily increasing or decreasingTrend of 6 up or 6 downInvestigate for tool wear, drift, or gradual process change
Rule 414 consecutive points alternating up/downZigzag patternCheck for alternating material lots or operator changeover
Rule 52 out of 3 consecutive points beyond 2σ (same side)2/3 points in zone A (same side)Investigate for shift — may be masking larger issue
Rule 64 out of 5 consecutive points beyond 1σ (same side)4/5 points in zone B or beyond (same side)Process average has likely shifted
Rule 715 consecutive points within 1σ of centerline (both sides)All in zone CStratification — check for mixed data streams
Rule 88 consecutive points beyond 1σ (either side)None in zone C for 8 pointsMixture — check for two separate processes being combined
