# Work Instruction: How to Use Demand Good QA Intelligence

**Document:** WI-DGQI-001
**Audience:** Paid subscribers (Foundation and Most Good)
**Purpose:** Give this document to any new user of your subscription (e.g., a new
teammate) so they can get productive without a live walkthrough.

> The same content is always available, tier-aware and printable, inside the app
> at **Dashboard → How to use (work instruction)**. That in-app copy reflects the
> reader's real plan and data; this file is a static copy for onboarding emails,
> printed binders, or offline reference.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This instruction explains how a subscriber signs in, reads the daily quality
pulse, uses the review generators, and — on the Most Good plan — manages
supplier risk, the live regulatory watch feed, and license/certification
renewals. It applies to every Demand Good QA Intelligence subscriber.

## 2. Signing in

1. Go to the Demand Good QA Intelligence site and select **Member login**.
2. Sign in (or create an account) using the **same email address** you used to
   purchase your Thinkific membership/subscription.
3. The dashboard checks that email against Thinkific for an active
   enrollment. If it isn't found, you'll see a "Connect your membership"
   screen — confirm you purchased a plan and are using the matching email,
   then try again.
4. Once verified, you land on the **Overview** dashboard automatically. You do
   not need to log in to Thinkific separately afterward.

## 3. Reading the Overview dashboard

| Section | What it tells you | Included in |
|---|---|---|
| Overall quality score / Compliant products / Open actions | Top-line scorecard for the current period, with change vs. the prior period. | Foundation + Most Good |
| Quality trend chart | Trend line so you can see whether the score is improving or slipping. | Foundation + Most Good |
| Open actions | Specific, prioritized corrective actions (High/Medium/Low) with a due date. | Foundation + Most Good |
| Weekly decision digest | Plain-language summary of what changed this week and why. | Foundation + Most Good |
| Supplier risk intelligence | Per-vendor risk score, trend arrow, and open issue. | Most Good only |
| License & certification alerts | Any license/certificate due for renewal or expiring soon. | Most Good only |
| Regulatory watch | Live updates pulled automatically from FDA, CDC, CMS, Federal DHS, and The Joint Commission. | Most Good only |

## 4. Generating a Management Review

1. From the sidebar or Overview dashboard, select **Management review**.
2. The page auto-fills your current quality scorecard, weekly performance
   highlights, and open corrective actions.
3. Fill in the blank **Decisions**, **Resource needs**, **Improvement
   opportunities**, and **Next review date** fields during your meeting.
4. Select **Print / Save as PDF** and choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's
   print dialog to keep a signed record.

Available on both Foundation and Most Good.

## 5. Generating a Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

1. Select **Quarterly review** from the sidebar or Overview dashboard.
2. Review the executive scorecard, quarter-in-review summary, and priority
   risks/commitments table.
3. Most Good members also see the supplier performance table for the
   quarter.
4. Complete the **Next-quarter commitments** section with your team's top
   priorities, resourcing decisions, and owners.
5. Print or save the page as a PDF to share with leadership.

Available on both Foundation and Most Good.

## 6. Using the License & Certification Vault (Most Good)

1. Select **License vault** from the sidebar to see every tracked company
   license and certification, its jurisdiction, reference number,
   expiration date, and status.
2. Each record has a renewal alert lead time of 30, 60, or 90 days. When a
   license enters that window it is flagged **Renewal due** or **Expiring
   soon** and appears automatically in the Overview dashboard's alert card —
   no manual check required.
3. Work expiring items in order of soonest expiration date.
4. Use the **Email renewal alerts** option to opt in or out of email reminders.
   Email reminders are sent in addition to the in-app alerts when the service
   email provider is configured.
5. Records shown today are sample data pending a document-storage
   integration; contact support to add your company's real licenses and
   certifications.

## 7. Reading the Regulatory Watch feed (Most Good)

This feed refreshes automatically (about once an hour) from FDA, CDC, CMS,
Federal DHS, and The Joint Commission — you don't need to check agency
websites yourself. Each item shows the issuing agency, an impact estimate
(High/Medium/Low, based on keywords such as "recall" or "warning letter"),
and a link to the original notice. Treat the impact rating as a starting
point for your own review, not a final regulatory determination.

## 8. Unlock Most Good (Foundation members)

Foundation includes your scorecard, trend, open actions, weekly digest, and
both review generators. Upgrading to **Most Good** adds Supplier Risk
Intelligence, the License & Certification Vault with renewal alerts, and the
live multi-agency Regulatory Watch feed. Upgrade from **Dashboard → Unlock
Most Good** or the pricing section on the site.

## 9. Exporting your data

- **Export Excel** (Overview dashboard) — a workbook of your metrics,
  actions, and weekly digest (plus Supplier Risk, License Vault, and
  Regulatory Watch sheets on Most Good).
- **Export PDF** (Overview dashboard) — a print-ready version of the same
  data you can save or share.

## 10. Getting help

If your membership isn't recognized, verify you signed in with the email
used at checkout, then contact support from the Demand Good QA Intelligence
site. The in-app version of this instruction (Dashboard → How to use) always
reflects your current plan and is the source of truth if this document goes
out of date.
