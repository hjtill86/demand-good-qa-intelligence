# Work Instruction: How to Use Demand Good QA Intelligence

**Document:** WI-DGQI-001  
**Audience:** Paid subscribers (Foundation and Most Good)

The printable, plan-specific copy is inside the app at **Dashboard → How to use**. Use that page when you give instructions to a teammate. This file matches the product as of the Part 11 validation release.

## 1. Purpose

A subscriber signs in, reads the quality pulse, keeps controlled documents, training, complaints and CAPAs, and approved supplier files, and downloads an auditor packet. Most Good also covers shared logins, the Part 11 validation the customer runs and signs, supplier risk, the license vault, and regulatory watch.

## 2. Signing in

1. Open https://demandgoodqa.com and select **Member login**.
2. Create an account or sign in. Use the email from checkout.
3. You land on the **Overview** dashboard.

## 3. Quality registers

Both plans include:

- **Controlled documents** — approve with your password. Approved and retired revisions stay on the register.
- **Training** — who was trained on which revision. A new revision shows retraining due.
- **Complaints and deviations** — saving one opens a CAPA. Closing it requires your password. A closed CAPA stays on the register.
- **Approved suppliers** — the approval, certificate, and expiration. Marking a file Approved requires your password, and that file then stays.
- **Audit trail** — who changed a record, the UTC time, and the previous value.
- **Auditor packet** — one workbook of those registers and the trail.

The review board, CAPA worksheet, audit-readiness pack, and SPC tools are working papers saved on the account. The complaint register is the record for a complaint.

## 4. Validation suite

The Validation suite drafts IQ/OQ/PQ steps on the screen. It is not saved on the account and it is not a validation of Demand Good QA. Print it if you want to keep the draft.

Most Good stores the Part 11 validation separately. Open **Part 11 validation**, run each test, write what you saw, and sign with your password. That signed revision stays on the account. Signing it records your test. It does not by itself make every record Part 11 compliant.

## 5. Most Good

- **Logins** — 5, including the subscriber. Five more are $500 a month. Each person has their own email.
- **License vault** — enter the license, upload the file, and set a 30-, 60-, or 90-day alert.
- **Supplier risk** — scores you enter in Manage data. They are separate from the approved-supplier files.
- **Regulatory watch** — public notices from FDA, CDC, NIH, WHO, CMS, DHS, The Joint Commission, and NABP. The impact label is a starting point, not a regulatory determination.

## 6. Exporting

**Export Excel** and **Export PDF** on the Overview cover the metrics, actions, and digest you entered. The **Auditor packet** is the quality-register workbook.
